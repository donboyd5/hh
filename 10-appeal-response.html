7  Who responded to the FY26 annual appeal?

The fall 2025 annual appeal was mailed to about 1,100 households — nearly all of them prior donors. A household responded if anyone in it gave a SUCCEEDED gift to the Annual Fund Drive - 2025-2026 campaign between October 1, 2025 and January 31, 2026. Giving and engagement are counted the same way as everywhere in this book: rolled up to the household, with engagement measured only for events that had happened before the appeal mailed (see Methodology).

import pandas as pd

arts_share_not = (not_appealed["eng_n_performance"] > 0).mean()
class_share_not = (not_appealed["eng_n_class"] > 0).mean()


def _fmt(raw: pd.DataFrame) -> pd.DataFrame:
    return raw.rename(
        columns={
            "households": "Households", "responded": "Gave", "rate": "Response rate",
            "dollars": "Total $", "avg_gift": "Avg gift", "median_gift": "Median gift",
        }
    ).assign(
        **{
            "Response rate": lambda d: d["Response rate"].map(pct),
            "Total $": lambda d: d["Total $"].map(money),
            "Avg gift": lambda d: fmt_gift(d["Avg gift"]),
            "Median gift": lambda d: fmt_gift(d["Median gift"]),
        }
    )


def display_summary(df, by, order=None, total=True):
    """response_summary for display, with a totals row (overall rate, responder-level
    avg/median — not averages of the group rows)."""
    raw = response_summary(df, by).set_index(by)
    if order is not None:
        raw = raw.reindex(order)
    parts = [_fmt(raw.reset_index())]
    if total:
        grand = response_summary(df.assign(**{"_total": "Total"}), "_total")
        parts.append(_fmt(grand.rename(columns={"_total": by})))
    return pd.concat(parts, ignore_index=True)

114 of 1,039 appealed households responded (11.0%), giving $33,995 of the $37,980 donated to the campaign during the window. The median responding household gave $100.

7.5 Households that were not appealed

Of the 4,598 households in the database, 3,559 were not appealed. The mailing list was overwhelmingly the donor file: 97.0% of appealed households had given before, versus 5.1% of those not appealed. Among the not-appealed, 48.6% had attended performances and 34.5% had taken classes before the appeal.
