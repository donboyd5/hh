8  How much does the annual fund drive raise?

Every fall Hubbard Hall mails an annual-fund appeal. Neon codes gifts to an Annual Fund Drive campaign for each drive back to FY13, which allows two measures that answer different questions and are worth keeping apart:

  • In the window — gifts dated October 1 through January 31 of the drive’s fiscal year, the four months after the letter mails: how the mailed appeal itself performed.
  • Total coded to the drive — everything ever coded to that drive’s campaign, whenever it arrived: what the drive ultimately brought in.

The fall 2025 drive’s in-window giving is examined household-by-household in Who responded to the fall 2025 appeal?; this chapter steps back to the year-by-year series. See Methodology for the clean-gift basis (business gifts coded to a drive, about $21k across all years, are excluded).

tab = tab.loc[tab.index <= pd.Timestamp.today().year]  # drop the drive not yet mailed

What the series shows:

  • The window measure is steadier and recently stronger. In-window giving averaged about r f"${tab.loc[tab.index <= 2019, 'oct_jan'].mean():,.0f}" across the fall 2012–2019 drives and r f"${tab.loc[tab.index >= 2020, 'oct_jan'].mean():,.0f}" across fall 2020–2025, on household counts that barely moved. The best window on record is the fall 2020 pandemic drive (r f"${tab.loc[2021, 'oct_jan']:,.0f}" from r int(tab.loc[2021, 'households']) households, with a largest gift of only r f"${tab.loc[2021, 'top_window_gift']:,.0f}").
  • The gap between the two measures is concentrated, not diffuse. In most years a single recurring five-figure gift arrives just after the window closes, and mid-decade drives carry sizable later-coded tails (spring gifts carrying the campaign label), so total-coded exceeds the window by anywhere from under ten percent to a factor of three.
  • The typical gift has not changed. The median in-window gift sits between $70 and $100 in every drive on record.
  • A separate Summer–Fall 2022 matching campaign (about $28k, FY23) ran alongside that year’s drive and is not included in these figures.
