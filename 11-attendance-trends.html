12  How many people attend our classes and performances?

Total attendance across classes, performances/events, and community gatherings, by fiscal year, with what those attendees paid in tickets and class fees. Attendance counts people — each registration’s ticket records — rather than registration counts (about 1.7 people per registration); the fiscal year runs July–June and is labeled by its ending year. See Methodology for the event categories, the headcount definition, and the cross-cutting fundraiser / youth / film flags. Class-taking households specifically are profiled in Who takes classes at Hubbard Hall?.

Code 
import pandas as pd
import plotly.express as px
from hh.io import read_parquet
from hh.viz import style, BLUE

regs = read_parquet("processed", "registrations_enriched.parquet")

from hh.analytics.attendance import attendance_by_fy
from hh.analytics.mailing import fiscal_year

tab = attendance_by_fy(regs)
current_fy = int(fiscal_year(pd.Series(pd.Timestamp.today()))[0])
complete = tab.loc[tab.index < current_fy]  # the current FY is partial

CATS = ["classes", "performances_events", "community"]
LABEL = {"classes": "Classes", "performances_events": "Performances & events",
         "community": "Community"}

attendees = complete[[f"{c}_att" for c in CATS]].astype(int)
attendees["Total"] = attendees.sum(axis=1)
attendees.columns = [*map(LABEL.get, CATS), "Total"]
attendees.index = [f"FY{fy - 2000}" for fy in attendees.index]
attendees.index.name = "Fiscal year"
ytd = int(tab.loc[current_fy, "total_att"])

Notes on these figures:

  • Attendees are people, not registrations — a family of four on one registration counts as four. Only attendees whose ticket status is SUCCEEDED are counted, so refunded canceled sections contribute nobody.
  • Dollars are registration revenue as recorded in Neon — ticket and class fees only, not donations, and gross: a canceled section keeps its refunded amounts (under $2.5k in the latest complete year).
  • A small share of attendance (363 attendees and $7,571 in FY26) is booked to Neon’s internal house accounts — real walk-up tickets, included here but attached to no household.
  • FY20–FY22 cover the pandemic period and its reopening.
  • Events carry cross-cutting flags alongside their category — fundraiser, youth program, and film — so, for example, a concert that is also a fundraiser can be counted either way; those flags do not change the category totals above.
